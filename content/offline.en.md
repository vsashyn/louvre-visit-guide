# Using this offline

## Put it on your home screen

Open this page in Safari, tap the share button, then Add to Home Screen. Chrome on the iPhone cannot do this, only Safari can.

Do it before you download the pictures, not after. An app launched from the home screen is treated differently from a tab you left open, and that difference is what stops the phone clearing 14 MB of pictures a week later when it wants the space.

From then on, open it from the icon rather than from Safari.

## Download the pictures

Go back to the list and tap Download on the Pictures card. About 14 MB, one picture at a time, a minute or two on wifi.

Do it at home. Museum wifi is the thing this whole app exists to avoid.

The card takes itself away when the set is complete. If something goes missing later, it comes back and offers to fetch only the gap rather than starting again.

## Check it before you go

Turn on airplane mode, force quit the app, and open it again from the home screen icon.

- Open any work and check the picture is there
- Switch to the other language and back
- Open the route and follow a link from one of the stops

Do this a day or two before the visit rather than on the morning of it. If the phone has cleared something, you want time to put it back.

## Inside the museum

Nothing reaches for the network once the guide is downloaded, so bad wifi cannot slow it down or answer half a request. Both languages are stored, so the switch works with no signal.

If a newer version exists, a bar offers it at the bottom of the screen. It is applied only when you tap it, and only when you are online. Nothing rearranges itself while you are reading.

If a picture is missing, the app says so when it opens and names the works, rather than letting you find out standing in front of one.
