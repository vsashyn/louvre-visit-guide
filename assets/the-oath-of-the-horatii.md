# The Oath of the Horatii

Jacques-Louis David, 1784–1785. Denon wing, Level 1, Room 702 (Salle Daru).

## In one line

Painted for Louis XVI five years before the Revolution, and it became the visual language of the people who executed him.

## What you are looking at

Three brothers stretch their arms toward their father, who holds up three swords. Behind and to the right, three women collapse into a curve of grief. Three stone arches divide the space, one per group.

Everything on the left is straight lines and tension. Everything on the right is curves and collapse. David is making an argument about duty and feeling, and he is not being subtle.

## The story

The subject comes from Livy. Rome and Alba Longa agree to settle a war by sending three champions each. Rome sends the Horatii brothers, Alba the Curiatii. One Horatius survives. His sister Camilla, betrothed to one of the dead Curiatii, weeps for her fiancé, and her surviving brother kills her for it.

David invented the oath scene. It is not in Livy or in any classical source. He needed a single image that compressed the whole story and he made one up.

Commissioned by the crown through Charles-Claude Flahaut de la Billaderie, the king's director of buildings. David painted it in Rome and exhibited it there first.

## Look for

- The three swords. The composition's vanishing point sits right at the father's hand.
- The women on the right. The one in white is Camilla, who will be murdered by her brother after the battle.
- The floor tiles, which establish the perspective grid.
- The absence of brushwork. David's surfaces are deliberately smooth and hard, a rejection of Rococo touch.

## Interesting facts

- The Pope asked for a private viewing when it was shown in Rome.
- Rival academicians hung it badly at the Paris Salon of 1785. Public complaint forced them to move it to a better spot.
- The three-armed salute here was later picked up as the "Roman salute", which is a modern invention with no ancient evidence, and from there passed into fascist iconography.
- A smaller 1786 replica, painted with his pupil Girodet, is in Toledo, Ohio.
- David went on to vote for the execution of the king who commissioned this painting.

## Talking point

Nobody in the picture is looking at the same thing. Trace each person's eyeline.
