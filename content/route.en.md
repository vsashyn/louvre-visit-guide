# One full day

## Entrance

Carrousel du Louvre, 99 rue de Rivoli, or straight off the Palais-Royal–Musée du Louvre métro. Underground, less obvious to tourists, and reported at 5 to 20 minutes through security against 30 to 60 at the Pyramid at 11:00. It takes groups as well as individuals, which is its one weakness, but the volume difference outweighs it.

If you pass the Porte des Lions and it is open, take it instead. Quai François Mitterrand, individuals only, no groups. Often no queue at all, and it opens directly into the Denon wing where the route starts, skipping the Hall Napoléon walk. It closes without notice when staffing is short, so treat it as a bonus, never as the plan. Last entry 17:00.

The Pyramid is the fallback. A timed ticket gets you the green priority queue there. Passage Richelieu is groups only.

## Shape of the day

- Denon, level 1, from 11:15 to 14:45
- Denon, level 0, from 14:45 to 15:15
- Sully, level 0, from 15:15 to 15:45
- Lunch, from 15:45 to 16:30
- Richelieu, all four levels, from 16:30 to 19:00

## If you are ahead of schedule

Four works the Louvre calls masterpieces sit here rather than in the route: the Seated Scribe, the Great Sphinx of Tanis, the Portrait of Louis XIV and the Card Sharp. Promoting any of them means trimming the Salon Carré block.

## If you are running out of steam

Drop in this order. Everything above the cut stays worth doing.

- Denon 706 to 709, down to Cimabue and Giotto only
- Galerie d'Apollon
- Richelieu 0, down to Hammurabi and the Lamassu
- Richelieu 2, down to the two Vermeers
